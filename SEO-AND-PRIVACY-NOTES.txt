DERBY STAR FC SEARCH VISIBILITY

This build includes:
- Search-friendly page title and description
- Relevant club-name and location terms
- Open Graph sharing metadata
- SportsTeam structured data for search engines
- robots.txt allowing search crawlers

Important launch steps:
1. Publish the site on a permanent domain you control.
2. Replace the placeholder domain in robots.txt and create a sitemap.xml using that exact domain.
3. Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.
4. Add the live website URL to Derby Star FC's Instagram bio and other official profiles.
5. Keep club name, Ota/Ogun State location, phone and Instagram consistent across official profiles.

PLAYER PRIVACY:
Player registration applications and personal contact details must remain private and must not be exposed to search engines. Only players formally approved by management should be considered for a public squad profile, and only with the player's informed permission. Do not publish date of birth, phone, email, home address or application records in public pages.

LIMITATION:
Search engines decide when and whether to index a newly published site. SEO metadata improves discoverability but cannot guarantee a ranking or immediate appearance. This static preview does not itself submit the site to search engines or provide a secure online registration database.
