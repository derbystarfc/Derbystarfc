Our History

Derby Star Football Club was established in 2024 in Ota, Ogun State, Nigeria. Built on passion, discipline, teamwork and determination, the club is committed to developing football talent and promoting competitive football.

Our Mission

To discover, develop and empower talented footballers through quality training, discipline, teamwork and competitive opportunities.

Our Vision

To become a recognised and respected football club in Nigeria and beyond, developing exceptional players and competing at higher levels.

Our Core Values

Discipline · Teamwork · Respect · Hard Work · Excellence
